Our Vision

To uplift and transform the Muslim society from their educational poverty in the light of true knowledge which leads one back to Allah


Who we are

We started out as a small group of likeminded individuals from the university Jamia Millia Islamia who wanted to do something for our society. We saw the poverty in the Muslim community and decided to do something to curb it from our end.

What we do

We sponsor students from financially weak Muslim families who are unable to afford their education. We specifically support students who have a desire to study but are unable to do so because of their financial situation.

Here's how you can help

There are multiple ways in which you can do your part to propagate this vision and do something for your Aakhirah


Become a donor

Our donors pledge a minimum amount of ₹500 every month for this cause. With their continued help we can predict how many long term students we can support at any given time. You can also choose to provide one time help, for example, buying books and uniforms for the students or paying one installment of the fees. To become a part of the community, click here.

Volunteer with us

Our ever growing group of students require an ever growing group of volunteers who are ready to mentor and guide them. If you are fond of kids and want to shape the future minds of our community, this is the role for you. To become a volunteer, you just have to fill a short Google Form and a member of our team will contact you for further details.

  2. AUGUST 2022

    A Tale of Determination

    Aarzoo is an intermediate passout (2022) student and lives in Muzaffarnagar. She is determined and an ambitious student and had scored 77.6% in Uttar Pradesh 12th Board Examination. By the will of Allah, she has gone through many ups and downs in her life. She lost her father about two years ago who was the only bread earner for the family.

    She aspires to become a doctor and become independent as soon as possible to support her family and fulfill their dreams. TIF is currently supporting her with NEET-UG preparation mentoring and coaching fees.

  3. JULY 2022

    The Blooming Lotus

    Sana is a student in class X studying in Shikhsha Matriculation high School in Chennai. Hers is a family too that is having a tough time recovering from covid. While she is a bright student, she faced significant losses in her studies due to inadequate digital provisions.

    Her father works as a cab driver and her mother used to due odd jobs to earn some money however, her health has deteriorated in the last 2 years because of which she cannot engage in other work. Her mother's recent hospital bills have strained family finances. TIF helps with Sana's educational costs because of which she can continue her studies with ease. We pray for the family's wellbeing and success!

  4. JUNE 2022

    New Beginnings

    Alina is an LLB student who hails from Muzaffarnagar, UP. She is a brilliant student with excellent performance in academics. She always dreamt of becoming a lawyer, but her dream was shattered when her father, the sole bread winner of the family, passed away due to a heart attack 13 years ago.

    Of all the compromises they made, education was not one of them. Although for her, it meant going from a private school to a Govt school. Ilm Foundation fully supports her educational expenses since May 2021. She aims to pursue LLM and become a judge in a few years and make the conditions of the Muslim community in UP slightly better. May Allah accept her and our efforts. Ameen!

  6. APRIL 2022

    Light at the End of the Tunnel

    Tasmiya Kausar just completed class X from Madrasa-e-Tarbiyatul Banat in Bangalore. She lives with her maternal grandparents. Her grandfather is a carpenter who helps with the daily needs. Her father left the family 9 years ago and hasn't contacted the family since then. After her father left, the family has run into financial troubles time and again.

    While her grandfather is able to provide for some financial relief they still need financial assistance especially for education which the group has supported. Her mother is a housewife and is in search for a tailoring job. She has 3 younger siblings. One younger sister and two younger brothers. All of them are enrolled at school.

    Tasmiya is a very hardworking student and has an excellent academic record. She aims to become a CS company secretary and so has taken up commerce CEBA combination as her pre-university combination. We pray that she succeeds with flying colours!

  7. MARCH 2022

    Fighting the Odds

    Alvina, Atif, Ayaz live in Atrauli, Uttar Pradesh with their mother Mrs. Shabana after the sad demise of their father. Alivina and Atif completed their senior secondary education in 2021. Ayaz studies in City Convent School, Atrauli in class 8th. The family owns a local kirana store that makes their ends meet to some extent.

    Being sponsored by the group, Alvina and Atif were aided in the preparation of various competitive exams for B.A, B.B.A, B.Com in Jamia Milia Islamia and Aligarh Muslim University in the form of books, application fees and a smart phone. Sadly not being selected, they still aim to start a graduation course through applying to various universities. Ayaz has been able to clear his class 8th exams. Despite the odds, Alvina and Atif remain motivated in pursuing a higher education. Their mother has faith in them and constantly pushes them to study better. We pray Allah SWT makes the journey easy for them!

  9. JANUARY 2022

    Little Battles

    Furqan Khan lives in Gaya, Bihar with his mother and younger brother. His mother works as a housemaid and works hard to make sure that Furqan studies well. He was previously enrolled at Banat Model school however the school did not provide quality education and Furqan managed through the successive lockdowns by going for tuitions.

    After being selected for sponsorship by the group, he was shifted to a better school. Furqan now studies in class 2 at Guardian Public School. Furqan is a passionate young boy who wants to study. He has some trouble with his studies from time to time perhaps due to the events in the last two years. But he shows positive signs and his mentor is working hard with him. We pray that Allah (SWT) makes the road ahead easy for him and his family.
