About Us

Who we are

We started out as a small group of likeminded individuals from the university Jamia Millia Islamia who wanted to do something for our society. We saw the poverty in the Muslim community and decided to do something to curb it from our end.

What we do

We sponsor students from financially weak Muslim families who are unable to afford their education. We specifically support students who have a desire to study but are unable to do so because of their financial situation.

Volunteer

Here's how you can help

There are multiple ways in which you can do your part to propagate this vision and do something for your Aakhirah


Become a permanent donor

Our permanent donors pledge a minimum amount of ₹500 every month for this cause. With their continued help we can predict how many long term students we can support at any given time.

Become a one time donor

Sometimes we get a case which needs immediate temporary help. For example, buying books and uniforms for the children or paying one installment of the fees. For such one-time support cases, we require funds which doesn't affect our long term permanent beneficiaries. Your one time donation is perfect for such situations.

Become a mentor

Our ever growing group of permanent students also require an ever growing group of volunteers who are ready to mentor and guide them. If you are fond of kids and want to shape the future minds of our community, this is the role for you.
