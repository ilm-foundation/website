Our Vision

To uplift and transform the Muslim society from their educational poverty in the light of true knowledge which leads one back to Allah


Who we are

We started out as a small group of likeminded individuals from the university Jamia Millia Islamia who wanted to do something for our society. We saw the poverty in the Muslim community and decided to do something to curb it from our end.

What we do

We sponsor students from financially weak Muslim families who are unable to afford their education. We specifically support students who have a desire to study but are unable to do so because of their financial situation.

Here's how you can help

There are multiple ways in which you can do your part to propagate this vision and do something for your Aakhirah


Become a donor

Our donors pledge a minimum amount of ₹500 every month for this cause. With their continued help we can predict how many long term students we can support at any given time. You can also choose to provide one time help, for example, buying books and uniforms for the students or paying one installment of the fees. Click here

Volunteer with us

Our ever growing group of students require an ever growing group of volunteers who are ready to mentor and guide them. If you are fond of kids and want to shape the future minds of our community, this is the role for you. To become a volunteer, you just have to fill a short Google Form and a member of our team will contact you for further details.
